Lines to 3D solids, blocks, hatches, dimensions.

02

Layer standards, drawing reports, schedules from blocks.

An IFC or a messy DWG, made into a clean drawing.

You stay in control

Nothing moves until you say so.

  1. Off until you press HeronNo chat can see your AutoCAD until you connect it.
  2. PadlocksChange, Publish and Admin each unlock on their own.
  3. Keep or DiscardCheck every change before you keep it.
  4. Local onlyThe link answers only your Windows user, with a new password each time.
