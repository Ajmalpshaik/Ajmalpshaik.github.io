Just ask

Say it. Watch it appear.

“Check this drawing against our layer standard.”
“Put sheet E-101 on A1 with our title block.”
“This IFC came in — make it a shop drawing.”

Same chat, same padlocks as Heron AI for Revit.

What it does

Drawing work, done for you.

01

Draw and edit

Lines to 3D solids, blocks, hatches, dimensions.

02

Check

Layer standards, drawing reports, schedules from blocks.

03

Sheets and PDF

Title blocks, viewports, plot to PDF.

04

Clean up imports

An IFC or a messy DWG, made into a clean drawing.

You stay in control

Nothing moves until you say so.

01

Off until you press Heron

No chat can see your AutoCAD until you connect it.

02

Padlocks

Change, Publish and Admin each unlock on their own.

03

Keep or Discard

Check every change before you keep it.

04

Local only

The link answers only your Windows user, with a new password each time.

In development · not released yet

Open source. Follow it on GitHub.

Please don’t install it yet — it is still passing its live checks in AutoCAD. Developers: ideas and corrections are welcome as an Issue.
