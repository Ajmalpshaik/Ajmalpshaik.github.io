AR Viewer

See a Revit model at real size in AR on your iPhone. Choose an FBX export, check the size, create the USDZ file, then tap View in AR.

Your file stays on your device. It is opened and converted inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared, and this page runs no analytics.

The AR Viewer needs JavaScript and a recent browser: Safari on iOS 16.4 or newer, or a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer.

What this does, and what it does not.

Scale. Revit writes FBX in feet, and the tool converts to metres before it makes the USDZ, so AR shows the model at real size. Check one known dimension before you rely on it.

Position. The model is centred and its lowest point sits on the floor. It is not placed at its site location or turned to face north.

Colours. Only materials saved inside the FBX come across. An FBX with none shows plain grey, and textures stored on the computer that made the FBX are skipped.

Faces. AR draws each face from its front side only, the same as the preview.

Size. In testing, a 27.6 MB Revit FBX made an 84 MB USDZ. If a phone struggles, create the USDZ on a computer, send it to the phone, and open it from the Files app.

Built with three.js 0.186.1 (FBXLoader and USDZExporter), loaded from jsDelivr. AR opens in Safari on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.4 or newer. Built by Ajmal P.S, MEP BIM Coordinator in Doha, Qatar. Found something wrong? Tell me and I will fix it.
