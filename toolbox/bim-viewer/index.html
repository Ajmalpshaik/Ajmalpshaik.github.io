BIM Viewer

Open a Revit model as IFC. Check every element's properties, levels and categories, cut a plan or a section, and measure. Then switch to the walk view and walk through the building as a robot, a soldier or X Bot, like a game.

Your file stays on your device. The IFC is opened inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared, and there is no server and no API key behind it.

The BIM Viewer needs JavaScript and a browser with WebGL 2: a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

      From Revit to here

      1. In Revit, open a 3D view and use File › Export › IFC. IFC 2x3 Coordination View 2.0 or IFC4 Reference View both work.

      2. Tick Export only elements visible in view to keep the file small. For a phone, export one level or one zone at a time.

      3. Drop the .ifc file on the viewer above, or press Open IFC. A browser cannot read a .rvt file, so it has to be IFC.

      Controls

      BIM view. Drag to turn, right-drag or middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom at the cursor. Click an element for its properties, double-click to zoom to it. Keys: H hide, I isolate, U show all, X x-ray, M measure, F fit, Esc clear.

      Walk view. WASD to move, mouse to look, Shift to run, Space to jump, C to swap first and third person, Q to change character, 1–6 for emotes. On a phone, use the stick on the left and drag on the right to look.

      What this does, and what it does not.

      Properties. Everything shown comes from the IFC: names, types, property sets and quantities, as the exporter wrote them. Revit's own parameter groups, like Identity Data and Dimensions, appear when the export includes them.

      Walls and doors. In the walk view you collide with what is visible. Doors and rooms are left out, so doorways are open. Hide a category in the BIM view and it stops blocking you too. Very large models collide with walls, floors, roofs and stairs only, to stay smooth.

      Position. The model is moved near the origin when it opens, so georeferenced exports far from 0,0,0 still draw cleanly. The sun is not set to the site's real north or location.

      Size. Everything runs on your device, so a big model needs a big device. If a file struggles, export less of it, or lower the Quality setting.
