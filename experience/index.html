Eleven years

Every project raised the cost of being wrong

A mistake in a villa is an afternoon. A mistake in an operating plant is a shutdown. The work did not just get bigger — it got less forgiving, and that is what pushed the tooling forward.

Three stops

In order, and what each cost

May 2015 – Jun 2024

BIM Modeller / MEP Draftsman

Powermech · Stream · Roots Energy · Mekdam · TEM-AC · Doha

Villas, towers, malls, hotels, offices and airport renovation works. Shop, coordination, builder’s work and as-built drawings, through consultant approval. Supervised drafting teams and coordinated daily with other disciplines. This is where the AutoLISP routines and Dynamo scripts started.

At stake: learning exactly where models break

Jun 2024 – Apr 2025

BIM Modeller · Arab Engineering Bureau

Qatar Private Schools Programme, PPP Package 2 · consultant side

Revit MEP models for HVAC, plumbing, firefighting and drainage across fourteen school buildings, federated on Autodesk Construction Cloud with clash detection in Navisworks. Coordination drawings, schedules and quantity take-offs produced straight from the model.

At stake: get the standard right once, or fix it fourteen times

Apr 2025 – Present

BIM Specialist & MEP BIM Coordinator · Trags

Polyethylene Plant, Ras Laffan · contractor side

Lead HVAC BIM modelling and coordination on an operating production plant, in Revit with BIM 360. Navisworks clash detection driven to closure with MEP, structural and civil teams, and LOD and project BIM standards enforced across the federated model at every milestone. The custom Revit tools I rolled out to the drafting team cut project timelines by an estimated 20%.

At stake: shutdown risk, not rework risk
