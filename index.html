About

Ten years of MEP BIM in Qatar

I take MEP models from concept through construction documentation to as-built, on both the consultant and the contractor side. My projects cover airports, petrochemical plants, schools, towers and mixed-use developments. Day to day that means Revit modelling, Navisworks clash detection, federated model reviews, and keeping LOD and project BIM standards enforced at every milestone.

The part that sets my work apart is what happens around the model. I build the automation my team runs on: Revit add-ins in C# and pyRevit, Dynamo scripts, AutoLISP routines, and an AI agent connected straight into a live Revit session. Repetitive drafting work goes to the tools, so the team's time goes to coordination.
