Questions

Straight answers

The six things people ask first.

01

Are you available for BIM roles in Qatar?

Yes. I am in Doha on a transferable QID with NOC and can join within days, for BIM Coordinator, BIM Modeller and MEP BIM Specialist roles anywhere in Qatar.

02

How do you run clash detection and get clashes actually closed?

Rule-based Navisworks tests per discipline pair, noise filtered out, issues grouped with an owner and a date in BIM 360 or ACC, and re-run every milestone until they are closed.

03

Can you set up BIM standards and a BEP for a new project?

Yes: template, shared parameters, worksets, ISO 19650 naming and an LOD matrix per milestone, plus model audits that catch drift before submission week.

04

How do you take over a model somebody else started?

Audit first, then fix. A short report of what is wrong and what it costs, then the fixes in order, structural problems first.

05

Can you build a tool for our team?

Yes, built around your own templates, parameters and naming. I will also tell you honestly if a template change would do the job better.

06

Do you take freelance or remote automation work?

Yes for automation, remote and documented. Modelling and coordination I do on site in Qatar.
