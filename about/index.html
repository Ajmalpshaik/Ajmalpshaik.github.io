The practical part

Where I am, what I hold, and what I actually do day to day. Doha, Qatar. Transferable QID with NOC. Able to join within days.

What I do

The model is the job. So are the tools that build it.

01

Concept to as-built

I take MEP models from concept through construction documentation to as-built — villas and residential towers, malls, hotels, schools, mixed-use developments, an operating airport terminal and a petrochemical plant.

Day to day that means Revit modelling, Navisworks clash detection, federated model reviews, and holding each submission to the standard the BEP actually asked for.

02

Automation built in

I write the software, not just the model. Revit add-ins in C# and pyRevit, Dynamo scripts, AutoLISP routines, and an AI agent wired into a live Revit session.

Repetitive drafting goes to the tools. The team’s time goes to coordination.
