The practical part. Concept to as‑built.

Where I am, what I hold, and what I actually do day to day.

What I do

The model is the job. So are the tools that build it.

Concept to as-built

I take MEP models from concept through construction documentation to as-built — villas and residential towers, malls, hotels, schools, mixed-use developments, an operating airport terminal and a petrochemical plant. Day to day that means Revit modelling, Navisworks clash detection, federated model reviews, and holding each submission to the standard the BEP actually asked for.

Automation built in

I write the software, not just the model: Revit add-ins in C# and pyRevit, Dynamo scripts, AutoLISP routines, and an AI agent wired into a live Revit session. Repetitive drafting goes to the tools — the team’s time goes to coordination.
