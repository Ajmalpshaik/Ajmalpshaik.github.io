Selected work

All in Qatar

Five projects that cover the range: industrial, institutional, aviation, residential and mixed-use. The models belong to the clients, so what is here is the scope, the role and the part that was hard — which is the part worth asking about anyway.

01

Polyethylene Plant, Ras Laffan

Industrial HVAC BIM on a live plant. Services share pipe racks with process lines, and access clearances are non-negotiable — every route is checked against structure, process and existing services before issue.

Petrochemical · ongoing · contractor side
02

Qatar Private Schools, PPP Package 2

Fourteen buildings delivered from one coordinated MEP type model. Get the standards right once and all fourteen stay consistent — get it wrong once and you fix it fourteen times.

Fourteen schools · consultant side
03

Doha International Airport

MEP renovation under a live operating terminal, with a deadline that could not move. Every route proven against survey data before fabrication, because there was no room to rework it on site.

Aviation · FIFA 2022 renovation
04

Madinat Mawater

MEP modelling and drawing production across several phases of a large mixed-use development, through every consultant submission.

Mixed-use · multiple phases
05

Union Residential Tower

Full MEP services modelled in Revit — riser coordination, builder’s work openings and as-built drawings through to handover. Plus market development MEP squeezed into tight ceiling zones on the New El Haraj Souq redevelopment.

High-rise residential · souq redevelopment
